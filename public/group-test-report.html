Turn a test results sheet into a management report

Upload the Excel export from your test platform. The page analyses participation, scores and section performance, and creates a PDF you can send as it is.

Drop the Excel file here .xlsx, .xls or .csv Choose file

The file is read inside your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Email addresses in the sheet are never shown in the report.

What the sheet needs
  • One row per student, with Name and Group columns. Member Id is used when present.
  • One column per test, with the maximum marks in brackets at the end of the heading, for example 345 - AptitudeTraining-Test-04 [non-proctored] (20).
  • Students who did not appear can be marked Absent or left blank.
  • Footer lines such as “Generated By” are ignored.
